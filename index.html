Bio

I am currently a Senior Researcher at the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, National Technical University of Athens, advised by Prof. Konstantinos Tserpes. I received a BSc in Computer Science in 2013 and an MSc in Web Engineering in 2015, both from Harokopio University of Athens. In 2022 I completed my PhD in Distributed Systems at the same department.

I am a member of the Efficient Computing Lab, specializing in efficient computing and data analytics (GitHub). My research interests span distributed computing, edge and cloud computing, machine and deep learning, AI robustness, efficient computing, big data management and analysis, and spatiotemporal / trajectory analysis.

I have participated in numerous EU-funded and national research projects.
